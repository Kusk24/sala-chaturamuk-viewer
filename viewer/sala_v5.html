The mask is view-inconsistent — it fires on some frames and not on others, so the same treeline is black in one photograph and textured in the next, and the model cannot satisfy both; that alone accounts for 2.40 dB, and removing it is exactly what the current model does. Beyond it, COLMAP split the capture into two disconnected sub-models, and only the larger was trained. The 51 photographs left out are not a random scatter — they are, every one of them, the Sala Inside folder. Standing under the roof, the camera sees painted ceiling and columns and almost nothing it saw from the terrace, so the interior never bound to the exterior walk and COLMAP filed it as a second, separate reconstruction. What you are looking at is therefore the exterior, built from 395 photographs; the interior exists as its own unlinked sub-model. The fix on the next capture is a handful of deliberate transition frames — standing in the doorway, turning slowly from outside to inside — so the two halves share features.
